Many transformation projects do not fail because ideas are missing. They struggle because natural systems, production systems, value chains, finance, governance, and markets are often designed in fragments.

This is why I developed the FlowScape Framework (FSF).

I am pleased to share the SSRN preprint of my conceptual paper:

"From Fragments to Systems: The FlowScape Framework for Multi-Scale Alignment of Natural Systems and Value Chains"

Key messages:

1️⃣ FSF is a systems-thinking and project-design framework.
It connects natural systems, agrifood production, circular resource flows, value chains, logistics, markets, governance, finance, digital systems, and enabling mechanisms across scales.

2️⃣ FSF can support integrated project conceptualization.
It can be applied to emerging areas such as agrifood systems transformation; landscape carbon, AFOLU mitigation, and carbon-credit generation; climate-smart agriculture; agroecology; nature-based solutions; sustainable agricultural landscapes; ecosystem restoration; digital MRV; traceability; circular economy; food loss reduction; resilient value chains; climate finance; investment-ready transformation pathways; regional cooperation; policy dialogue; and regional public goods.

3️⃣ FSF can help integrate modern design approaches.
These include One Health, the Water–Energy–Food–Ecosystems (WEFE) Nexus, Integrated Landscape Management, nature-positive approaches, Just Transition, Locally Led Adaptation (LLA), gender-responsive climate finance, and Public–Private Partnerships (PPPs).

This is a preprint version and has not yet been peer-reviewed. I am sharing the framework figure as a visual synthesis and would welcome comments, suggestions, and opportunities for discussion with colleagues and partners working on integrated project design in these areas.
